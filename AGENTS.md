# LANA development

For every program change, follow `.loop/PROTOCOL.md` and read
`.loop/checkpoint.md` before continuing. Existing installed applications,
credentials, production services, GOLD and KRAKEN are outside the local pilot.
Do not uninstall the app, reset its data, or create a second package.

Record evidence rather than treating compilation or workflow success as proof
of device behaviour. Never include secrets or private conversation text in logs.
